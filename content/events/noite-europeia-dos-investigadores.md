The European Researchers’ Night is celebrated on the 28th September. UNIDCOM and IADE will mark the occasion with a series of workshops. The program prepared by UNIDCOM/IADE aims to provide a meeting with research and researchers, professors and students, in the areas of Design and Visual Culture.

### Night Photography Workshop

Requirements for participation: Digital camera with manual settings and, if possible, tripod.

Objectives: To know and understand the digital camera mechanism, the operation of the photometer, the choice of white balance and the influence of ISO. Understand the basics of editing, namely, how to “reveal” the digital file (Raw) with Photoshop.
