DRIW - Design Research and Innovation Week aims to establish itself as a festival to promote design research and innovation through design. As the name suggests, it is a multi-event, week-long initiative. Among the most common events are colloquia, round tables, exhibitions, book launches and workshops, among others.

This year's edition will take place from May 3rd to 9th.
