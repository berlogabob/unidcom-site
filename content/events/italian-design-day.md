Together with the Italian Embassy in Portugal, UNIDCOM/IADE organize the webinar “Aerospace Design: Technology is Beauty.”

The initiative is held as part of the Science and Technology Week “Ciência Viva” on the occasion of the Italian Design Day 2020.

With the participation of Italian and Portuguese designers and scientists, the webinar focuses on the role of Design in the aerospace industry and the research of new materials, technologies and approaches.

The official language of the event will be English.
