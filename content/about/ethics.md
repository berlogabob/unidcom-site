---
title: "Ethics"
description: "The principles that guide research at UNIDCOM/IADE."
aliases:
  - "/centre/ethics/"
---

Research at UNIDCOM/IADE should respect the people, communities, environments, and cultural materials involved in it. Researchers are responsible for explaining their methods, obtaining appropriate consent, protecting personal information, and reporting their work accurately.

Ethical questions begin when a project is defined and continue through collection, analysis, publication, and preservation. The level of review and documentation should reflect the people, data, and possible effects involved.

Researchers should raise uncertainty early and seek review before work begins when a project involves participants, sensitive data, vulnerable groups, or a meaningful risk of harm. Research integrity also requires clear attribution, transparent conflicts of interest, and care in the use of images, archives, and generated material.
