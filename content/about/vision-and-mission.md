UNIDCOM/IADE advances knowledge in design and communication. The unit supports research that is rigorous, open to collaboration, and attentive to the social and material conditions in which design and communication operate.

Its mission is to create the conditions for researchers to work across disciplines, develop research with public and private partners, and make results available through publications, projects, teaching, and public activity.

The unit treats design and communication as connected fields of inquiry. It brings theory and practice into the same research process and supports work at different scales, from individual studies to long-term collaborative programmes.
