UNIDCOM/IADE members manage their own record through the researcher portal. What appears on this website — your profile, your publications, your project affiliations — is what you confirm there, once it has been approved by the unit.

<p><a class="nav-login" href="{{< portal "/login" >}}">Researcher login</a></p>

## What you can do in the portal

- **Confirm your profile.** Name, biography, photo, ORCID iD and Ciência ID. Submitting it for review moves it from draft to pending, and it appears on this site once the unit approves it.
- **Manage your selected publications.** Works synced from ORCID arrive as candidates; you decide which are yours and which five to highlight on your profile.
- **File support requests.** The three request types below, tracked from draft through to the Board's decision.
- **Read the Welcome Pack.** Email signature, affiliation and FCT statements, logos, forms, contacts. It is [open without signing in]({{< portal "/app/welcome/start" >}}).

## Signing in

Sign-in uses your **ORCID iD**. There is no separate password to remember, and no account to create — the portal recognises the iD already recorded against your UNIDCOM profile.

If your ORCID iD is not yet on file, sign-in will fail. Send it to <unidcom@iade.pt> and it will be registered.

## Support requests

All support requests require approval from the UNIDCOM Board. The Support Request Form (FPA) must be filed **at least four months in advance**, dated and signed. Support requests for activities that are not in your semester activity plan may not be considered.

### DPD — conferences and publications

Travel, registration and open access fees for conferences, workshops and exhibitions. Attach the event programme, the paper or poster acceptance notification, a copy of the accepted manuscript with the affiliation and FCT statement, and a detailed provisional budget.

### Open Access

Scientific translation, scientific revision, APC fees, submission fees and publication fees. Articles must be in full open access, with a mandatory DOI on the final publication, and payment by bank transfer only. Check whether the journal is predatory at predatoryjournals.org and use the Think. Check. Submit. tool.

### Missions

Travel for coordination meetings, supervision meetings, scientific organisation, proposal preparation and fundraising. Integrated researchers only. The mission report must be submitted within 15 days of completion.
