UNIDCOM/IADE works with researchers, doctoral students, institutions, and organisations whose questions connect with design and communication.

Available calls, research positions, doctoral opportunities, and invitations to collaborate will be published on this page. Each notice will state its scope, eligibility, timetable, and contact point.

For a general enquiry, use the details on the contact page and describe the research question or form of collaboration you have in mind.
